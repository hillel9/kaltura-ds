Modal

For forms and other user tasks. It is static: clicking outside it or pressing Escape does not close it, so people don't lose what they typed. It closes only with a Cancel button (any data-ds-close element) or DS.closeModal(). The title and footer stay in place; the body scrolls when the content is tall. Open with DS.openModal('id') or a data-ds-open="id" button. Focus is trapped while open and returns to the opener.
